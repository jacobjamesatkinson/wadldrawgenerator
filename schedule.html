Round & venue

Venue options come from Tabbycat room names for the selected round (e.g. H2.15 → prefix H2.). Tabbycat room numbers are not used for row labels — add your own room list in the allocation table.

Room allocations & preview

Pairings with bye, withdrawn, retracted in a team name, or debate status postponed, are omitted automatically. Debates fill each timeslot top-to-bottom in division order; edit room labels and adjudicator in the table below. Drag a debate cell onto another to swap them (or onto an empty cell to move). Swaps survive page reloads until you change round/venue or click Reset manual swaps.

When checked, each timeslot gets its own adjudicator column in the preview and export. When unchecked, one adjudicator applies to the whole room row.

Load a round and venue to preview.

Bulk add rooms

Paste room codes, then click Add rooms from list. One per line or comma-separated. Range on one line: H2.15-H2.35. Keeps rooms already in the table; skips duplicates.

Bulk assign adjudicators (CSV)

Paste CSV with room,adjudicator (header row optional). Rooms must match the table. Example: H2.15,Eugene
