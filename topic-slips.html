Rooms (debate & prep)

One room list for the venue. Rows are debate rooms per timeslot; prep rooms are assigned automatically from the same list. Prep starts one hour before each debate timeslot — so during 5.15 debates, rooms hosting those debates cannot be used for 6.15 prep (e.g. a Novice debate in Room1 blocks Junior prep there). At 5.15 only, Affirmative teams may prep in their debate room; at 6.15 and 7.15, and all Negative teams, prep uses a separate room from the list.

Load metadata → round → venue, then import rooms.

Import rooms (bulk)

Paste room codes for this venue — used for debate allocation rows and as the pool for prep rooms. One per line, comma-separated, or range H2.15-H2.35. Click Add rooms from list to merge, or Replace with autofill series to generate enough rooms for debates + prep (count is automatic).

Autofill numbered series (replaces list)

Replaces the whole room list and clears manual debate swaps. Room count is computed from your draw (debate rows + prep capacity).
